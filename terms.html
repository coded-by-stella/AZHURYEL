4. Legal basis

  • Handling your request: legitimate interest and or steps prior to a service agreement, depending on the situation.

We store personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. If you contact us but do not engage further, we periodically review and delete older data. If you opt in to marketing messages, we keep your data until you unsubscribe and for a limited period after to document your preference.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal data with third parties for their marketing. If we use service providers for email, form delivery or hosting, they only process data on our instructions.

7. Your rights

  • Access, correction and deletion of your data.

To exercise your rights, email hello@azhuryel.com.

We use essential cookies only to ensure the website works correctly. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies at this time. See the Cookie Policy for details.
