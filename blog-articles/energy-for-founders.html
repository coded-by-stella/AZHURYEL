Founders often manage time and ignore energy.That is why they collapse at night and wake up with a mind already loaded. Energy management is leadership infrastructure.

The trap: adrenaline as a business model

Adrenaline feels productive, until it turns into irritability, sleep disruption and emotional flatness. You can still “win” while your body is quietly declining.

The 3 energy leaks that destroy performance

Leak 1: Constant access

Notifications turn your nervous system into a permanent alert mode.

Leak 2: Unfinished loops

Your brain stays “on” because nothing is parked with a next step.

Leak 3: Emotional suppression

Leaders often store stress in the body.Then it leaks into tone and choices.

A simple energy system that holds

1) One protected window daily

Choose a 60 to 90 minute block for deep work or decisions.No notifications.

2) Two response windows

Batch communication twice a day.This reduces noise without harming speed.

3) One body anchor

Walk, strength or breathwork 20 minutes daily.You regulate state, not feelings.

4) One evening closure ritual

Park open loops with next actions.This stops the night mind spiral.

5) One relationship boundary

Choose one hour of real presence daily.This protects home from becoming a spill zone.

Quick win: the 3-line shutdown

End your day with three lines in notes:

  • Tomorrow’s first move
  • One decision I am delaying on purpose
  • One boundary I will enforce

This creates safety for your brain.You are not forgetting.You are parking.
