Is this Academy for you

The Azhuryel Holistic Academy is not a casual collection of courses. It is a professional path for people who want to honour their calling with depth, ethics and structure.

You will feel at home here if

  • You feel called to work in holistic fields, but want a grounded, ethical and serious formation.
  • You are sensitive, empathic or neurodivergent and traditional high pressure environments exhaust you.
  • You want a method that unites energy work, nervous system regulation and coaching instead of random techniques.
  • You are ready to do your own inner work while learning how to safely support others.

It is not your path if

  • You want a quick “weekend certificate” without practice or supervision.
  • You are not open to self reflection and inner work.
  • You are only interested in collecting titles rather than embodying the work.
  • You do not feel ready to take responsibility for holding space for others in the future.

If your heart is in service and your mind needs structure, the Academy is built to support both.

What the Azhuryel Academy really is

The Azhuryel Holistic Academy is the educational heart of the Azhuryel Rebalance Path. It is a fully online professional school that turns your sensitivity, intuition and lived experience into a structured, accredited holistic profession.

You do not simply learn techniques. You move through a coherent method that always integrates three dimensions:

Personal healing

You regulate your own nervous system, process your stories and learn to live from balance rather than burnout.

Technical skills

You train in Reiki, Chakra Healing, breath, meditation, emotional release and coaching with clear structures and safety.

Professional identity

You build confidence, ethics and basic business skills so you can actually work with clients in a sustainable way.

Lessons are a blend of recorded classes, live online gatherings, mentoring calls and individual appointments for attunements and exams.

Your 3 Year Professional Map

The Academy is organised in three clear years. Each year has a focus, a rhythm and specific certifications. The detailed curriculum will be shared during your Academy interview and in the official guide.

🌱 Year 1 • Rebalance Yourself

You cannot hold others if your own system is collapsing. Year 1 focuses on your nervous system, your energy and your everyday balance.

  • Foundations of nervous system regulation, breath and meditation.
  • Introduction to energy anatomy and chakra awareness.
  • Reiki Practitioner training (first levels) with online attunements.
  • Holistic lifestyle basics: rhythms, nourishment, simple herbal support.
  • Emotional awareness, boundaries and self worth foundations.

Goal: move from survival mode to a stable inner ground so you can study and serve without burning out.

💎 Year 2 • Rebalance Others

Once your base is stable, you learn how to work with clients using energy, breath and emotional tools inside a safe, structured method.

  • Reiki and Chakra Healing advanced levels.
  • Breathwork and Meditation Teacher foundations.
  • Crystal and subtle body practices within the Azhuryel framework.
  • EFT / TFT Emotional Release tools for regulation and stress relief.
  • Introduction to coaching skills: presence, questions, holding space.

Goal: start practising with real people in guided settings, with supervision and feedback.

🕊️ Year 3 • Mastery and Holistic Leadership

Year 3 integrates everything into a clear professional identity. You refine your specialisation and learn how to bring your work into the world.

  • Reiki Master level and advanced Chakra Healing training.
  • Meditation and Breathwork Teacher consolidation.
  • Life, Relationship and Neurodiversity Coaching integration.
  • Ethics, boundaries, documentation and client safety modules.
  • Holistic business basics, branding, time mastery and mission design.
  • Supervised practice and case study review before final certification.

Goal: leave with a clear professional profile, ethical grounding and the confidence to hold deep work with clients.

The full official curriculum and timetable will be shared with prospective students during the Academy interview and through the Academy Guide.

How you study inside the Academy

The Academy is fully online and designed for real life. You study in a way that respects your nervous system while still moving forward in a disciplined, professional way.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience in holistic practices

No. The Academy is designed to guide you from foundations to advanced levels. Previous experience can help, but it is not mandatory. What matters most is your commitment to the path.

Can I study while working or caring for my family

Yes. The structure combines recorded lessons with scheduled live sessions. You will have clear timeframes and support to integrate study into real life, not escape from it.

Is everything really online

Yes. All lessons, live calls, attunements, mentoring and exams are conducted online through secure platforms, so you can attend from anywhere in the world.

When will I receive the full curriculum and tuition details

The complete program structure, schedule and financial details will be shared with you during the Academy interview and in the official written guide you receive after applying.

What if I have ADHD, OCD or anxiety

The Academy is designed with emotional regulation and neurodivergent realities in mind. You will find micro structures, repetition, gentle pacing and practices that respect sensitive systems.

What is the next step if I feel called

Your next step is to apply for an Academy interview. Together we will explore your situation, your vision and whether this is the right path and timing for you.
