What Reiki actually is

Reiki is a Japanese healing art developed in the early 1900s by Mikao Usui. It is based on a simple understanding: when your natural energy flow becomes blocked or weak because of stress, trauma, emotional pressure or fatigue, your whole system struggles. The body becomes tense, the mind becomes loud, emotions become overwhelming or numb, clarity disappears and vitality fades.

Reiki works by restoring this natural energetic flow, allowing your system to reset gently and safely. It is not imagination and it is not suggestion. It is the body remembering how to function without constant tension.

How a Reiki session feels

Most people describe it as a warm, calm wave moving through the body. The mind slows down naturally without effort, the chest softens, the breath deepens and there is a sense of quiet inside that they have not felt in years.

Some people feel tingles, lightness, emotional release or simply deep rest. Your body decides what you need most in that moment.

Benefits you can expect

  • Less anxiety and mental overactivity
  • Relief from emotional overwhelm and inner pressure
  • Softening of physical tension (jaw, shoulders, stomach tightness)
  • Improved sleep quality and deeper rest
  • A stronger sense of connection with yourself
  • Support with burnout and chronic fatigue
  • More clarity, focus and emotional stability

In the Azhuryel Rebalance System, Reiki is used to calm the nervous system, restore emotional balance, clear energetic stagnation and support a more grounded daily rhythm.

Why Reiki works in a practical way

When the body feels safe, it resets. When energy flows, the mind quiets. When the nervous system calms, clarity returns. Reiki communicates safety to your entire system. That is why many people say:

“I finally felt calm without forcing myself to calm down.”

It is the difference between trying to relax and your body remembering how to relax on its own.

EFT Emotional Release, when feelings sit in your body and will not move

Do you feel anxiety in your chest, a knot in your stomach or a constant pressure in your throat even when nothing “bad” is happening right now?

Maybe your thoughts race, your heart beats faster and your body reacts as if there is danger even when you are just at home, at work or trying to sleep.

These are signs that your nervous system is carrying unresolved emotional charge. EFT is a gentle way to help your body feel safer again.

What EFT actually is

EFT, Emotional Freedom Technique, is a mind body method where we tap lightly on specific acupressure points on the face and upper body while you focus on what you feel or remember.

It combines elements of modern psychology with ancient meridian work to calm the stress response and reduce the emotional intensity linked to certain memories, fears or situations.

You stay conscious and in control the whole time. We simply invite the body to release what it no longer needs to hold.

What an EFT session feels like

We start by naming what you feel right now. It can be a physical sensation, an emotion or a specific situation that triggers you. Then, as you speak, you tap gently on points like the side of the hand, top of the head, eyebrow, under eye, under nose, chin, collarbone and under arm.

Most people notice the number they give their emotion (for example “9 out of 10”) dropping step by step. The body softens, breathing deepens and the charge around the situation decreases.

Signs EFT can help you

  • You feel anxiety in the chest or pressure in the throat
  • You carry tightness in the stomach when you are stressed
  • Your thoughts race at night and you cannot switch them off
  • You react strongly to small triggers and feel ashamed after
  • You feel stuck in fear, guilt or anger even when you understand the situation logically
  • You have memories that still bring a strong emotional reaction
  • You feel on edge, jumpy or constantly “on alert”

What EFT helps you shift

Emotions are not just in the mind. They sit in the body as tension, heat, numbness or discomfort. EFT gives your body a way to process them instead of storing them.

  • Reduces the intensity of anxiety and stress
  • Softens physical tension linked to emotions
  • Helps you feel safer and more present in your own body
  • Supports sleep by calming the nervous system
  • Releases emotional charge from specific memories or fears
  • Builds a practical tool you can use on your own after the session

Why you should book now

When your nervous system stays in survival mode for too long it affects your mood, your relationships and your ability to make clear decisions.

If you feel that your body is carrying more than it can hold, EFT offers a practical and gentle way to release that weight step by step.

You do not have to keep fighting your own reactions. You can learn to work with your body instead of against it.
