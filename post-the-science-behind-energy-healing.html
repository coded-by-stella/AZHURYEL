The Science Behind Energy Healing

By Stella Marrali • October 27, 2025 • 5 min read

Your body runs on electricity. Every heartbeat, thought, and emotion carries a measurable signal. When stress stacks up, those signals lose coherence and the system feels noisy.

Energy as information

The nervous system is an interpreter. It reads signals and decides whether to tense or release. Energy healing practices use attention, breath, and intent to restore orderly signaling so that your body can switch from survival to regulation.

The biofield in practice

Most clients report warmth, tingling, or a softening of breath. These are indicators that tone is shifting toward balance. Better sleep, better digestion, and steadier focus follow when regulation becomes the default state.

Try a 2 minute coherence reset

  • Hand to heart, slow inhale for 4, slow exhale for 6.
  • Imagine breath-light expanding from the chest in waves.
  • Notice shoulders, jaw, and belly settling into ease.

Treat energy as information and the body responds with clarity.
