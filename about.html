Why I created the Azhuryel Rebalance Path

I did not grow up in balance. I grew up in a dysfunctional family with a narcissistic and violent father and a mother who was emotionally dependent and distant. I learned very early to people please, to overadapt and to ignore my own needs.

For years I lived with anxiety, panic, emotional overload and deep fatigue. I felt invisible, unsafe and never enough. I kept functioning on the outside while my inner world was collapsing.

At one point the pain became too heavy. I realised that if I did not choose myself, my body and my mind would give up for me.

That was the moment I decided to rebuild my life instead of collapsing inside it.

    I know how it feels to live with:

  • Chronic anxiety and constant overthinking
  • Panic attacks and emotional exhaustion
  • Symptoms similar to ADHD and OCD
  • Weight fluctuation, stress and burnout
  • Years in toxic and narcissistic relationships
  • Fear of abandonment and low self worth

If you recognise yourself in these words, you are not broken. You are out of balance and that can change.

From self healing to a clear method

My own healing did not happen overnight. It started in 2013 when I chose a natural holistic path instead of numbing my pain. I began receiving energy sessions and working with gentle practices that calmed my system one layer at a time.

Reiki, Chakra work, ThetaHealing®, Meditation, Breathwork, herbal support and nervous system regulation tools slowly brought me back to presence. Panic softened, my breath deepened and I could feel my body as a safe place again.

What once helped me survive became the foundation of my purpose. I wanted to understand these disciplines deeply, to bring science and spirituality together and to offer others a clear map instead of scattered techniques.

Since 2015 I have dedicated my life to study and practice so I could guide others through the same shift from chaos to coherence.

The Azhuryel Rebalance Path

All the disciplines I studied were distilled into one framework. The Azhuryel Rebalance Path connects mind, body and energy through three main pillars.

Regulate

You learn nervous system tools and breath based practices that reduce stress spikes, calm your body and create mental clarity.

Release

You process stuck emotions and outdated patterns so you stop looping in the same stories and can respond instead of react.

Rebalance

You restore healthy energy flow through Reiki, Chakra work, Breathwork and grounded coaching so your daily choices match your truth.

The method is held in micro structures. Short repeatable practices you can integrate into real life so balance does not stay an idea but becomes a rhythm your whole system remembers.

Is Azhuryel the right path for you

People usually find me when they are tired of trying to fix themselves alone and are ready for a gentler, more structured way.

You will feel at home here if

  • Your mind feels loud and your body is often tense or exhausted
  • You are sensitive, intuitive or neurodivergent and need a safe pace
  • You want both emotional support and practical tools you can use

This work is not a match if

  • You want quick fixes without any self reflection
  • You are looking for pure prediction or fortune telling
  • You are not open to exploring your patterns with honesty

My role is not to save you. My role is to walk beside you while you remember your own strength and rebuild trust in your inner rhythm.
