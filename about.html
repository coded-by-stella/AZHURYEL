Why I created the Azhuryel Rebalance Path

I did not grow up with strong emotional safety or a clear sense of stability. Like many people, I learned early to adapt, stay strong and put my needs last.

Over time, that pattern can become expensive. It often shows up as anxiety, emotional overload, chronic fatigue or the feeling of always being “on” even when life looks fine from the outside.

My turning point was simple: I realised I could not think my way out of what my nervous system was carrying. I needed a grounded method that worked with the body, emotions and energy together.

That is how my personal healing journey became a professional path and eventually the foundation of Azhuryel.

Today I support clients who want to work through experiences such as:

  • Chronic stress and nervous system overload
  • Anxiety, overthinking and emotional fatigue
  • Repeating relationship patterns and low self worth
  • Burnout, transitions and identity resets
  • Sensitivity, overwhelm and difficulty feeling grounded

If you recognise yourself here, you are not broken. Your system is asking for support, structure and a safer inner rhythm.

From self healing to a clear method

My work is built on one principle: transformation becomes stable when it is embodied. This is why the Azhuryel method combines emotional processing, nervous system regulation and energy work instead of relying on mindset alone.

Over the years I studied and practiced complementary holistic disciplines to create a clear, grounded framework that is gentle, but real. A structure people can follow without feeling lost.

Professional standards that hold your journey

I am a Certified Holistic Therapist and an IAOTH Accredited Trainer with more than 10 years of experience and over 2000 hours of accredited study across complementary holistic disciplines.

My work is recognised by international organisations that maintain strong ethical and educational standards in holistic practice.

Your healing journey should never feel vague or unsafe. This work is held with compassion, empathy and clear structure.
